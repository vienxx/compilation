Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This application (the "App") is a private tool operated by an individual for personal use. It helps the operator prepare videos and publish them to YouTube channels that the operator owns or manages. It is not offered to the public as a service, and it does not collect information from members of the public.

What the App does with Google user data

The App uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services. When the operator connects a YouTube channel, Google asks for permission and the App receives an access token for that channel. The App uses that access only to:

  • upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and set their privacy status;
  • read the channel's basic details, its videos and their public statistics;
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for those videos, such as views, impressions and watch time;
  • read and reply to comments on those videos, where that permission was granted.

The App does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with any third party. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What is stored

  • Connection tokens. The OAuth refresh token for each connected channel, kept in a private database so the App can act on the channel without asking for sign-in every time.
  • Channel and video details. The channel's name and id, and for each video the App publishes: its id, title, description, length and the daily statistics YouTube reports for it.
  • Comment replies. The text of comments the App replied to and the reply it posted, so a comment is never answered twice.

Nothing else from a Google account is read or kept. The App does not access contacts, email, files or any Google service other than YouTube.

How long it is kept

Connection tokens are kept until the channel is disconnected in the App or access is revoked at Google, and are then deleted. Video records and statistics are kept for as long as the operator keeps the App, for the operator's own records.

Security

Data is stored in a private, access-controlled database and transferred only over encrypted connections. Only the operator can reach it.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's permissions page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, or by disconnecting the channel in the App. Revoking access deletes the stored token.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the operator also agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies

The App does not use advertising or tracking cookies. A browser session keeps the operator signed in to the App itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and the new text is published at this address.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the Google consent screen for this App.
